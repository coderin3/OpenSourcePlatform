Cinque Terre means 'Five Lands'.
It refers to the five beautiful villages located at the cliffs of the Ligurian coast in the northwestern Italy.
Also, it has been designated on UNESCO.

It's hard to find exact location, but it seems like Waitakero Rangers in New Zealand's North Island Auckland.

Aurora is a natural phenomena mainly observed in the Earth's polor regions.
Especially, it is observed in Norway, Iceland, Finland, Sweden, Canada, and more.

Peyto Lake is located at Rocky Mountains in Canada.
It is popular with Emerald colored water and its shape, which looks like wolf's head.
This beautiful color is created by fine rock particles, known as "glacial flour".
